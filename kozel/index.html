The trump suit lies under the deck

козырь

After dealing, the next card is turned face up and slid under the deck, sideways, so everyone can see it. Its suit is the trump (козырь) for the whole deal. If it's a heart, every heart beats every card of the other suits, even a 6♥ against an A♠.

Tap a suit to see that deal's trumps.

The bottom card is a real card

It stays in the game. It's the last card drawn from the deck, so whoever draws last gets that trump into their hand.

Changes every deal

Next deal, a new card goes under the deck and may set a different trump suit.

Same order in every suit: A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7, 6. I kept the 10 above the king, as in most point games. guess

03

Card points

очки
CardPoints

Points don't depend on suit or on being a trump. A king is 4 whether it's a trump or not.

11 + 10 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 30 per suit
30 × 4 suits = 120

After the last trick everyone counts their pile (a pair counts together). 61 or more wins the deal.

Sixes through nines are worth nothing. Adding the sixes doesn't change the 120 total.

04

Dealing and playing a trick

раздача и взятка
  1. Deal 4 cards to each player. guess Turn the next card face up under the deck as the trump. The rest is the stock.
  2. One player leads a card. The others play in turn, clockwise.
  3. You must follow the led suit if you can.
  4. If you can't follow, play anything: a trump to take the trick, or a worthless card to throw away. guess
  5. The highest trump wins. With no trump in the trick, the highest card of the led suit wins.
  6. The winner takes the cards into their pile. Everyone draws back up to 4, winner first, and the winner leads next.
  7. When the stock runs out, play out your hands. The face-up trump is the very last card drawn.

Example trick · hearts are trump · 4 players

When hearts are trump, the 6♥ takes the A♠ if the player had no spades to follow with. The lowest trump still beats the highest plain card.

Scoring: who becomes the goat

кто козёл guess

Whoever loses a deal gets penalty points: a player in a 2–3 player game, or a pair with four players. The first to collect 12 loses the game and is the goat.
