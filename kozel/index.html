01

The table

стол и пары

Four people play as two pairs. You sit across from your partner, so play alternates between the teams.

Each pair keeps one shared pile of the tricks it wins. Only the card points in that pile count, not how many tricks you took.

Play moves clockwise. The deal passes to the next player each round.

2- and 3-player versions exist, but this page covers the classic game for four.

02

Trumps: 14 cards that beat everything

козыри

This is the part that surprises people who know Durak. In Kozel the trumps are fixed and never change: all four queens, all four jacks, and every club. A queen of hearts is not a heart. It's a trump.

Plain suits простые масти

Spades, hearts and diamonds without their jacks and queens. Six cards each. Note that the 10 outranks the king.

Inside the trumps

Queens beat jacks. Within each, the suit order is ♣ › ♠ › ♥ › ♦. After the jacks come the remaining clubs: A, 10, K, 9, 8, 7.

Any trump beats any plain card, even a 7♣ against an ace of hearts (in the version where 7♣ isn't top, it's still a trump).

03

Card points

очки
CardPoints

Points don't depend on suit or on being a trump. A queen is 3 whether it's ♣ or ♦.

11 + 10 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 30 per suit
30 × 4 suits = 120

After the last trick each pair counts its pile. 61 or more wins the deal. 60 : 60 is a tie.

Sevens, eights and nines are worth nothing, but they're useful for following suit cheaply.

04

Dealing and playing a trick

раздача и взятка
  1. Deal 8 cards to each player, the whole deck. (Some tables deal 4 each and refill from a stock after every trick. See the checklist.)
  2. The player left of the dealer leads any card.
  3. You must follow the led suit. If a trump was led, you must play a trump. If a plain suit was led, play that plain suit.
  4. If you can't follow, play anything: a trump to take it, or a worthless card to throw away. You are not forced to trump.
  5. The highest trump wins the trick. With no trump in it, the highest card of the led suit wins.
  6. The winner's pair takes the 4 cards into its pile, and the winner leads next.

Example trick

Common mistake: diamonds are led and you hold K♦ and Q♦. You must play the K♦. The Q♦ is a trump, not a diamond, so it doesn't count for following. If you held only Q♦ and 8♠, you'd be "out of diamonds" and could play either.

05

Scoring: who becomes the goat

кто козёл

The losing pair of each deal gets penalty points. The first pair to collect 12 loses the game and is called козлы, the goats.

+2Normal lossLosers took 31–59 card points.
+4Under 31Losers took 30 or less. Often called losing "without eyes" or «без 30».
+6No tricks at allLosers didn't win a single trick.

60 : 60 is "eggs" («яйца»). Nobody gets penalty points, and in many groups the loser of the next deal gets double. The simplest version gives a flat +2 for every loss and ignores the tiers. Pick yours in the checklist.

06

House rules I've seen

местные правила

Queen of clubs meets 7♣

In the version where 7♣ is the top card: if Q♣ and 7♣ land in the same trick from different teams, the deal stops right there and the pair that played Q♣ gives the other pair 4 penalty points.

No trump lead to start

The pair that opened the previous deal may not open the new one with a trump.

Refill from the stock

Deal 4 cards each, put 16 face down. After each trick every player draws one card, winner first, until the stock runs out.
