02

Two cards under the deck

козырь и скрытая карта

Deal 4 cards to everyone. Then two special cards go under the deck: one face up, which sets the trump (козырь), and one hidden at the very bottom, which can change the trump at the end.

START OF THE DEAL

The face-up card's suit is trump. With 9♥ there, every heart beats every card of the other suits.

SECOND-TO-LAST CARD

The face-up 9♥ is drawn like a normal card into someone's hand.

LAST CARD

Whoever draws the hidden card shows it right away. Its suit is the new trump for the rest of the deal. If it's also a heart, nothing changes.

Trumps

Highest on the left

Every other suit

Same order in every suit: A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7, 6. The 10 beats the king.

03

Card points

очки
CardPoints

Points don't depend on suit or on being a trump.

11 + 10 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 30 per suit
30 × 4 suits = 120

The winner of a turn takes all the cards on the table, including face-down ones, with their points.

04

Playing a turn

как ходить и бить
  1. Lead 1 to 4 cards of the same suit, for example 7♠ A♠. Trumps can be led too (all trumps).
  2. The next player answers with the same number of cards. To win, they beat each card one by one, with a higher card of the same suit or any trump. They choose which.
  3. If they can't beat every card, or don't want to, they put the same number of cards down face down. They lose the turn, and those cards go to the winner.
  4. With 3 or 4 players, each next player must beat the cards that are currently winning, not the first ones.
  5. In pairs, if your partner is already winning, you can throw face down. Throwing high-point cards gives your team the points.
  6. The winner takes all the cards. Then the winner draws first and the others draw clockwise back up to 4. The winner leads next.
  7. Near the end, if there aren't enough cards for everyone to reach 4, the remaining cards are split evenly.

Four trumps (четыре козыря)

If you hold 4 trumps, you may put all four down at any moment, even out of turn or in the middle of someone else's turn. It's optional.

Any cards already led go back to their owners' hands. Everyone else must answer your 4 trumps: beat them card by card with higher trumps, or throw 4 cards face down.

05

End of a deal: bad points

штрафные очки

When all cards are played, everyone (or each pair) counts their card points. Below the safe line you get bad points. Whoever reaches 12 first loses the game and is the goat.

All the rules on one card

  1. 36 cards, 6 to Ace. Order: A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7, 6.
  2. 2, 3 or 4 players. With 4: two pairs (partners opposite) or everyone alone.
  3. Deal 4 cards each. Under the deck: one face-up card (trump) and one hidden card at the very bottom.
  4. Trump beats all other suits. The face-up card is drawn second-to-last.
  5. The hidden card is drawn last and shown right away. Its suit becomes the new trump.
  6. Lead 1–4 cards of one suit (trumps allowed).
  7. Answer with the same number of cards: beat each one (higher same suit or any trump, your choice) or throw face down.
  8. Beating is optional. Later players must beat whatever is currently winning.
  9. Winner takes all cards, including face-down ones, draws first, and leads next.
  10. Four trumps can be played at any moment, optionally. Others must beat them or throw face down.
  11. Card points: A 11, 10 10, K 4, Q 3, J 2, others 0. Total 120.
  12. Bad points by player count (see table). No cards at all = +6.
  13. 60 : 60 = eggs. The next deal's bad points are doubled.
  14. First to 12 bad points is the goat.
  15. Each game starts with a colour or suit pick and a random card to decide who goes first. After that, the winner of the last turn starts.
